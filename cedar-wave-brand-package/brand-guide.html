Positioning

Cedar Wave Advisory is a Victoria, BC-based advisory that brings frontier AI capability to small businesses, professionals, and community organizations — without losing sight of the fact that success isn't measured in dollars alone. Grounded in real operational and consulting experience, forward-facing on tools most people haven't touched yet.

Mission

Cedar Wave Advisory exists to make frontier AI capability practical, accessible, and grounded for the people who don't usually get first access to it — small businesses, independent professionals, and the communities they serve.

Elevator pitches

Short

Cedar Wave Advisory brings frontier AI experience and solid business fundamentals to small businesses and communities on Vancouver Island — practical, grounded, and never just about the bottom line.

Long

Most AI advice is either hype without substance or theory without application. Cedar Wave Advisory is different: hands-on experience building and running AI agents and automation, paired with real business strategy and operations fundamentals (MBA-level, not buzzword-level). Based in Victoria, BC, and built around a simple belief: the outcome that matters isn't only financial.

About

Cedar Wave Advisory was built on a simple idea: the tools driving the next decade of business shouldn't be locked away in big firms and big cities. We bring frontier AI experience — real, hands-on work building agents and automation, not secondhand theory — together with solid business fundamentals, to help small businesses, professionals, and organizations move forward with confidence. We measure success across three lines: financial, social, and environmental — because the right outcome is rarely just the dollar figure.

Service pillars

AI Coaching & Literacy

One-on-one coaching for individuals and professionals who want to actually understand and use tools like Claude and GPT — in their work, their business, or their own growth. Practical, hands-on, no jargon.

AI & Innovation Advisory

For organizations: adoption assessments, training-needs analysis, and group training, all through an innovation lens. Find out where AI fits your organization — and where it doesn't.

Core Business Consulting

Traditional strategy, operations, and planning support — plus project management for innovation, research, or novel initiatives. The foundation everything else builds on.

Engagement model: a free discovery call to start, then whichever format fits — fixed workshop fees, a package, or hourly.

Don't

  • Overclaiming or vague superlatives
  • Corporate-speak, filler adjectives

Background

Forest green carries the brand. Cedar brown grounds secondary text and dividers. Terracotta is reserved for calls-to-action and highlights only — used sparingly, it stays a pop rather than a dominant color.

Typography

Fraunces — headlines
Inter — body text. Used for paragraphs, labels, and UI copy across the site. Clean, highly legible, and quiet enough to let Fraunces carry the personality in headlines.

Logo usage

Primary lockup — light backgrounds

Reversed lockup — dark/forest backgrounds

Icon-only — favicon, social avatar, small placements

Minimum clear space: leave space equal to the icon's radius on all sides. Don't recolor the mark outside the approved light/dark pairs. Don't stretch or skew — scale proportionally only.
