## Page copy

### Home — hero
**Headline:** Cedar Wave Advisory
**Tagline:** Grounded in place. Built for what's next.
**Short pitch:** Cedar Wave Advisory brings frontier AI experience and solid business fundamentals to small businesses and communities on Vancouver Island — practical, grounded, and never just about the bottom line.

### About (full company story)
Cedar Wave Advisory was built on a simple idea: the tools driving the next decade of business shouldn't be locked away in big firms and big cities. We bring frontier AI experience — real, hands-on work building agents and automation, not secondhand theory — together with solid business fundamentals, to help small businesses, professionals, and organizations move forward with confidence. We measure success across three lines: financial, social, and environmental — because the right outcome is rarely just the dollar figure.

### Services — intro
Most AI advice is either hype without substance or theory without application. Cedar Wave Advisory is different: hands-on experience building and running AI agents and automation, paired with real business strategy and operations fundamentals. The result is practical guidance — whether you're an individual learning to work with tools like Claude and GPT, an organization figuring out where AI actually fits, or a business that just needs solid strategy and project management.

### Services — the 3 pillars
1. **AI Coaching & Literacy** — One-on-one coaching for individuals and professionals who want to actually understand and use tools like Claude and GPT — in their work, their business, or their own growth. Practical, hands-on, no jargon.
2. **AI & Innovation Advisory** — For organizations: adoption assessments, training-needs analysis, and group training, all through an innovation lens. Find out where AI fits your organization — and where it doesn't.
3. **Core Business Consulting** — Traditional strategy, operations, and planning support — plus project management for innovation, research, or novel initiatives. The foundation everything else builds on.

### Services — engagement/pricing note
Every engagement starts with a free discovery call. From there, pricing is flexible: fixed workshop fees, package pricing, or hourly — whatever fits the scope. Reach out and we'll figure out the right fit together.

### Differentiators (use as a features/why-us section anywhere on the site)
- Hands-on, early-access experience with frontier AI (agents, automation) — not theory, not reselling someone else's playbook
- A triple bottom line lens: financial, social, environmental — success isn't just revenue
- Strong business fundamentals (MBA-level) as the enabler of effective AI, strategy, and outcomes — not hype
- Local, Victoria/Vancouver Island-rooted — knows the community it serves, scales to org/gov work without losing that grounding

## Voice & tone (for any new copy — Field Notes posts, microcopy, error states, etc.)
**Do:** plain language over jargon; confident, not salesy; warm but direct; honest about limits ("here's where I'd bring in someone else").
**Don't:** buzzwords/AI hype-speak; urgency tactics ("act now"); vague superlatives; corporate filler.
